# Paul Tobiloba brand artwork

Selected direction: C from the second logo concept board, approved in the chat. The open P, shaped T crossbar and rounded geometric lettering are authored as native, font-free SVG geometry. These are vector interpretations of the chosen direction, not bitmap crops.

The same canonical geometry in `src/data/brandGeometry.json` produces the animated header, monogram, wordmark and icon. The font-free artwork does not require a downloaded typeface. Palette: graphite #090e0c, pale green #a5f3b8, off-white #edf4ee.

Original concept reference: `paul-tobiloba-concepts-v2.png`, generated with the built-in image_gen tool and retained with its exact prompt under the design workspace’s `output/paul-tobiloba-logo/` directory. The website uses the SVG masters rather than the concept bitmap.

Files: wordmark.svg (light), wordmark-ink.svg (dark), monogram.svg, icon.svg, favicon-32.png, apple-touch-icon.png, icon-512.png. PNGs are exports of icon.svg using the repository’s installed Sharp renderer.
